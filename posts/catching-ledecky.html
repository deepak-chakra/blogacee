Can Anyone Catch Ledecky?

by Ahna Chakra July 31, 2026

Though Katie Ledecky is still on top, new competition is catching up. Australia's Lani Pallister and Canada's Summer McIntosh are both close competitors of Ledecky. This 2025–26 season, Ledecky has been barely squeaking by, and this year's Pan Pacs will put all three swimmers to the test.

Will Australian swimmer Lani Pallister be able to clock Ledecky's LCM 800 free world record too? Pallister already beat Ledecky's SCM world record in the 800-meter freestyle. Meanwhile, Summer McIntosh is the only person to beat Ledecky in the LCM 800 freestyle in a decade, and she's still only 0.95 seconds away from tying Ledecky's world record.

All three of these swimmers are expected to clash in this highly anticipated race. But the real question is: who will be in the lead? Will Ledecky keep a strong hold on her winning streak? Will Australia grab hold of the 800 freestyle? Or will a new generation of swimmers take the lead?

My guess is that Ledecky will drop off, McIntosh will tie Ledecky's old time, and Pallister will place third.
